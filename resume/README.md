# Resume

[![Discord](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@intergrav/devins-badges@3/assets/cozy/social/discord-singular_vector.svg)](https://discord.gg/Qds5QMrnDv)
[![Ko-fi](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@intergrav/devins-badges@3/assets/cozy/donate/kofi-singular_vector.svg)](https://ko-fi.com/creeperkatze)

Resume shows you where you left off while your world or server loads, with a screenshot of the moment you quit as the loading screen.

## ✨ Features

**📸 Screenshot**

When you leave a world or server, Resume takes a screenshot of the world without the pause menu or the HUD.

**⏳ Loading screen**

The next time you join, the screenshot is the background of the loading screens instead of the panorama.

**🌅 Fade**

Once the world has loaded, the screenshot fades into the game, so you pick up right where you were.

## 🔧 Settings

Turn it on or off for singleplayer worlds and servers, and change how dark the screenshot is and how long it takes to fade. The screenshots are saved in the `resume` folder of your game folder. Delete one to get the normal loading screen back.

## 🌐 Translating

Translations are managed on [Crowdin](https://crowdin.com/project/creeperkatze-mc-mods). You can contribute without any technical knowledge, just pick your language and start translating.
