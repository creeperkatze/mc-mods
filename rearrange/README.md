# Rearrange

Drag and drop to reorder your servers, worlds, resource packs and data packs.

Grab a row and drag it to where you want it. The list scrolls when you hold it near the top or bottom edge. Dragging is paused while a search filter is active.

- **Servers** are saved to `servers.dat`, like the vanilla move buttons do.
- **Resource and data packs** can be reordered in the selected list. Packs with a fixed position stay where they are.
- **Worlds** keep your order in `config/rearrange.json`. New worlds show up at the top.
