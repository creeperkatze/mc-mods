Organize lets you sort your resource packs into folders and browse them right in the pack screen. No more scrolling through one endless list.

![Showcase](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/JC5JBj2W/images/3dff875190595904bd2c5dec3dc121d32ea7fd62.gif)

## ✨ Features

**📁 Folders**

Add folders in your `resourcepacks` folder and put packs inside. Folders can be nested as deep as you like.

**🧭 Browsing**

Folders show up above the packs in the available list, along with how many packs they hold. Click a folder to open it and use Back to go up again.

**✅ Selecting**

Shift-click a folder to select all of its packs at once.

**🖼️ Icons**

Put an `icon.png` in a folder to give it its own icon.

## 🌐 Translating

Translations are managed on [Crowdin](https://crowdin.com/project/creeperkatze-mc-mods). You can contribute without any technical knowledge, just pick your language and start translating.
